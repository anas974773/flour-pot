Our Philosophy

Direct-Mill Sourcing & Purity

At Flour Pot, we believe in minimal intervention. Instead of warehousing spices and oils for months, we mill our organic rice, dried red chillies, and coriander seeds directly at our local mill and pack them immediately. Our coconut oil is cold-pressed to ensure every jar preserves its natural aroma and health benefits.
